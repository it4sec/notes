### 4. Onboard buses, terrestrial networking, interfaces and standards

**Spacecraft buses/interfaces:** **CAN, SPI, I2C, UART, RS-485, SpaceWire, Ethernet, MIL-STD-1553, PC/104**. The paper specifically identifies these as common spacecraft subsystem interconnects. 

**Terrestrial/network technologies:** **IP, API, VPN, VoIP, BGP, OSPF, RIP, PPP, SNMP, Ethernet, cellular networks, Internet/Intranet, remote-access services**. 

**Standards/framework-related technologies:** **3GPP, CCSDS, ITU, ISO 27001, AS9100, PHY/MAC, OSNMA, FHSS, DSSS**.

**Commercial ground infrastructure named in the paper:** **AWS Ground Station** and **Azure Orbital**, both given as examples of cloud-based GS/GSaaS.
